# 02 Roadmap upkeep and epic pick
Keep `Plan-Roadmap` a short sketch of what we want next and why, and choose what comes next. Element rules: [roadmap](../elements/roadmap.md), [queue priority](../elements/board/queue-priority.md).

**Entry:** an epic reached Готово; or before the first take from an epic the owner has not chosen; or intake changed the plan; or the weekly audit found the roadmap stale.

**Who:** the orchestrator proposes, the owner confirms.

**Steps**
1. Read the sketch. Skim the open epic's «Ожидания» only when the next pick would otherwise contradict them. Do not turn the page into a specification.
2. Propose the next epic, or a parallel set when streams barely overlap ([parallel work](../project.md#parallel-work), [owner question](../templates/owner/owner-question.md)).
3. After the owner confirms: set epic «Приоритет» to the agreed sequence; raise that epic's open Очередь children to contiguous low numbers; for a confirmed parallel set interleave their children, still ahead of standing-backlog children.
4. Update `Plan-Roadmap` when reality changed ([plan pages](../elements/wiki/plan-pages.md)). An opened epic is a row. A goal not yet an epic stays one sketch line.

**Output:** confirmed next epic or set, priorities set, roadmap page current.

**Exit gate:** owner confirmation recorded; the top of Очередь is the chosen epic's work. Do not open the next roadmap epic from «Приоритет» alone. The owner may override any order explicitly; that instruction is not a standing rule.
