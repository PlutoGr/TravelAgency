# Plan- pages
Only two: `Plan-Product-Vision` (goals, roles, what the site must give, non-focus, success signs) and `Plan-Roadmap` (a short sketch: what is next and why; an opened epic is a row, anything else is one line). Template: [plan page](../../templates/wiki/plan-page.md). Roadmap rules: [roadmap](../roadmap.md). Do not grow `Plan-Roadmap` into a specification.

- No new plan or TZ pages: new work is an `Epic-` or `Feature-` page. Obsolete Plan-MVP pages stay deleted.
- The roadmap reflects reality (big MVP, operators table). Epic order is only the «Порядок» column; the roadmap carries no status words other than the content marks «сделано» and «отменено» (the stage is the issue label). An implemented item is marked «сделано» with a link to `Doc-` and the PR; a cancelled one «отменено» with the reason.
- Changing content or order: owner decision; recording a made decision: the orchestrator.
