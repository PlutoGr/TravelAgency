# Plan- pages
Only two: `Plan-Product-Vision` (goals, roles, what the site must give, non-focus, success signs) and `Plan-Roadmap` (now, open epics, until the big MVP, operator integrations, after MVP). Template: [plan page](../../templates/wiki/plan-page.md). Roadmap rules: [roadmap](../roadmap.md).

- No new plan or TZ pages: new work is an `Epic-` or `Feature-` page. Obsolete Plan-MVP pages stay deleted.
- The roadmap reflects reality (big MVP, operators table). An implemented item is marked «сделано» with a link to `Doc-` and the PR; a cancelled one «отменено» with the reason.
- Changing content or order: owner decision; recording a made decision: the orchestrator.
