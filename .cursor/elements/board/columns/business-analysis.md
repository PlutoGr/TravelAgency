# Бизнес-анализ (`статус: бизнес-анализ`)
- **Applies to:** epic only.
- **Enter:** take from Очередь.
- **While here:** the orchestrator and the owner fix «Ожидания» (a visual epic also gets an approved static mock). A cloud analyst is only a narrow helper. The orchestrator publishes ([Бизнес-анализ](../../../lifecycle/03-epic-business-analysis.md)).
- **Leave:** → Архитектура when the stage gate passes and the owner has approved (`approve BA #NN`) ([Бизнес-анализ](../../../lifecycle/03-epic-business-analysis.md)).
- **Owner question:** stay in this column; record the `Question` in [memory](../../memory.md#question); do not advance until the answer is recorded.
- **Who moves:** orchestrator, with the stage comment.
- **Required:** «Ожидания» state the exit result and what is out; a visual epic names «Эталон». A legacy «Бизнес и сценарии» counts until the page is refreshed.
