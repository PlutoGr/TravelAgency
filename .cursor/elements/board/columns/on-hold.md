# Удержание (`статус: удержание`)
- **Applies to:** all. Not part of any product sequence. Used for a **long hold of the work stream** (owner stop of an item or epic), not for an ordinary owner question.
- **Enter:** with the [hold comment](../../../templates/github/status-comments.md) saying why, what resumes it and which column to restore. Hold only by label. Record it in [memory](../../memory.md#question) in the same turn: `Question` = `hold: <reason>; resume: <condition>; restore «<column>»`, `Next` = restore that column after the resume word. The [Question guard](../../memory.md#question-guard) blocks every transition, so the never-stall loop never restores it; only the recorded resume turn does (guard exception (b)). A `hold:` is never re-asked by the silence policy.
- **Pausing an epic:** `статус: удержание` on the epic; no child of a held epic is taken, and its Очередь children get no «Приоритет» (intake included); clear «Приоритет» on its Очередь children; cancel every child cloud agent; move every open in-flight child (any column after Очередь) to Удержание, each with its own restore column named in its own hold comment. The epic's hold comment lists the paused children. Nothing resumes without a recorded owner resume.
- **Owner question during a stage:** do **not** use this column. Keep the issue in its current column, record the `Question` in memory, ask the owner ([silence policy](../../../orchestrator.md#8-never-stall)).
- **Leave:** to the recorded column, or Очередь with «Приоритет»; an owner-paused item only after the recorded owner resume.
- **Restart in the resume turn:** after restoring the label and Status, each restored row gets `Next` = the unfinished work of that column and `Waiting on` = `none` (a mechanical wait that still holds, such as `Deploy` or `issue #NN`, is kept); relaunch the stage agent when its deliverable was not accepted (then `agent`), and perform the row in that same turn.
- **Resuming an epic:** the recorded resume turn clears `hold:` on the epic and on every listed child carrying that hold ([Question guard](../../memory.md#question-guard) exception (b)); it restores the epic and also restores each child listed in the epic hold comment to the column recorded in that child's hold comment, and sets «Приоритет» again on the epic's Очередь children ([queue priority](../queue-priority.md)).
- **Who moves:** orchestrator.
