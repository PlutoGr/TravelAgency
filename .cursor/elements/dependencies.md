# Dependencies (Dependabot)
Dependabot PRs have no issue and keep default branch names; commit prefixes per [commits](git/commits.md).

The wake is the aggregated `pr-checks` event ([events](../lifecycle/events.md#webhook-match)). `author` `dependabot[bot]` marks it. Cursor Automations are not required checks and are not part of `result`.

- **Red** `result`: do not merge. Open a bug issue from the [bug template](../templates/github/bug-feature-issue.md) under the [standing backlog](../project.md#standing-backlog) ([bugs](../lifecycle/bugs.md)). The queue is not stopped. No review row.
- **Green** `result`: record the PR in [memory](memory.md#waiting-on-values) with the Dependabot row shape. If Automations are already finished, `Waiting on` = `none`, `Next` = full review, and review in that turn. If not, `Waiting on` = `Automations`, `Next` = full review when Automations finish. Do not poll in the turn.
- **Completion path:** the wakes that recheck `Automations` are a later `pr-checks` event for that PR head, weekday memory-refresh, and the Monday audit. When Automations are finished, switch to `Waiting on` = `none`, `Next` = full review, and review in that wake. **Never merge before that switch.** Review and merge are subject to the [Question guard](memory.md#question-guard).
- After merge: `Waiting on` = `Deploy` with the merge in the [Sha cell](memory.md#sha-cell); the [row lifecycle](memory.md#row-lifecycle) deletes the row on its green Deploy.
- **npm and NuGet** (minor, patch and major; majors are separate PRs, groups are minor/patch only): after the required checks and Automations finish, the orchestrator does a **full review** and may squash-merge without a separate owner yes when everything is green and clear. A peer conflict or an unclear breaking change: do not merge; open or take a [backlog](../project.md#standing-backlog) item through the FSM, or decline the PR with a reason, or ask the owner. A red `pr-checks` result is the bug issue above, not a second path. After every merge, notify the owner ([merge notice](../templates/owner/merge-notice.md)).
- **docker and github-actions** (including grouped updates that may contain majors): owner «да» before merge, recorded as the Dependabot row's `Question` (`<ask> (asked: chat)`) until answered; no merge while it is set ([Question guard](memory.md#question-guard)). The «да» is needed because merge deploys to dev with SSH secrets and CI does not validate image or action supply chain the way app build and tests do.
- Merge order rules apply (one at a time, green Deploy between).
