# Roadmap
The wiki page `Plan-Roadmap` (with `Plan-Product-Vision`) sets the coarse sequence of epics. It is not a task and not a task type. Page rules: [Plan- pages](wiki/plan-pages.md).

- **Responsible:** the orchestrator keeps it true; the owner decides content and order.
- **Update when:** an epic reaches Готово (move it to done, note what is next); the owner changes scope or order; intake adds a new goal the owner accepted; the weekly audit finds it out of date. Procedure: [roadmap upkeep](../lifecycle/02-roadmap.md).
- **Order of epics:** the roadmap and `Epic-` «Связи с другими эпиками»; then field «Приоритет» ([queue priority](board/queue-priority.md)).
- Changing the order or scope is an owner decision; reflecting a decision already made is orchestrator hygiene.
