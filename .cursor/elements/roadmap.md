# Roadmap
The wiki page `Plan-Roadmap` (with `Plan-Product-Vision`) is a short sketch of what we want next and why, in large strokes. It is not a specification, not a task and not a task type. Page rules: [Plan- pages](wiki/plan-pages.md).

- **Shape:** sketch lines until an epic is opened. An opened epic is a row (why, order). A finished epic is marked and drops out of «сейчас». Do not grow the page into a novel.
- **Responsible:** the orchestrator keeps it true; the owner decides content and order.
- **Update when:** an epic is opened or reaches Готово; the owner changes scope or order; intake adds a goal the owner accepted; the weekly audit finds it out of date. Procedure: [roadmap upkeep](../lifecycle/02-roadmap.md).
- **Order of epics:** the roadmap and `Epic-` «Связи с другими эпиками»; then field «Приоритет» ([queue priority](board/queue-priority.md)).
- Changing the order or scope is an owner decision; reflecting a decision already made is orchestrator hygiene.
