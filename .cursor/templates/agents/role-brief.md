# Role brief (structure of every file in briefs/)
Every role brief in [briefs/](../../briefs/README.md) has these sections, in this order. A launch prompt ([launch brief](launch-brief.md)) points the agent to one role brief.

```markdown
# <Role>
## Who you are
<seniority, mindset, quality standard, what you never allow yourself>
## Used for
<columns and jobs; links to lifecycle stage files>
## Read first
<project docs, rules, templates this role needs; only those>
## Bar
<what a correct deliverable must satisfy; the stage exit gate>
## Boundaries
<what this role must not do, on top of the common forbidden list>
## When blocked
<when to stop and raise a QUESTION; what not to work around>
## Deliverable and report
<what is returned, in which template, in which language>
```
